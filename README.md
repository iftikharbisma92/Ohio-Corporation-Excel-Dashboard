# 📊 Ohio Corporation Sales Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview
This project showcases an interactive sales dashboard developed in Microsoft Excel as part of a hands-on practice and assessment project, to analyze sales performance, expenses, and gross profit across multiple stores. The dashboard transforms raw business data into meaningful insights through interactive visualizations and KPI reporting.

![Dashboard Preview](Corporation%20Dashboard.PNG)

## 🎯 Business Objective
- Analyze overall sales performance.
- Compare sales, expenses, and gross profit across stores.
- Monitor key business KPIs.
- Support data-driven decision-making.

## 🛠️ Tools Used

- Microsoft Excel
- Pivot Tables
- Pivot Charts
- Data Cleaning
- Dashboard Design
- KPI Cards

---

## 📈 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

The dashboard tracks the following business metrics:

| KPI | Value |
|------|---------|
| Total Sales | $13,014,291 |
| Total Expenses | $6,122,386 |
| Gross Profit | $5,043,096 |

## 📊 Dashboard Components

### 1. Sales by Store

A horizontal bar chart comparing sales performance across all store locations.

**Insights Provided:**
- Highest performing store
- Lowest performing store
- Sales comparison between locations

---

### 2. Expenses by Store

A pie chart showing the distribution of expenses among stores.

**Insights Provided:**
- Expense contribution by each store
- Cost allocation analysis
- High-expense locations

---

### 3. Gross Profit by Store

A pie chart visualizing profit contribution by each store.

**Insights Provided:**
- Most profitable store
- Profit share by location
- Profitability comparison

---

### 4. Sales by Category

A bar chart displaying values across financial categories such as:

- Sales
- Salaries & Wages
- Rent Lease
- Operating Expenses
- Marketing & Advertising
- Inventory
- Gross Profit
- Cost of Goods Sold
- Accounts Receivable
- Utilities

**Insights Provided:**
- Major revenue drivers
- Cost structure analysis
- Expense category comparison

## 📌 Key Insights

- Columbus Flagship generates the highest sales contribution.
- Store profitability varies significantly across locations.
- Expenses are concentrated in a few major stores.
- Sales remain the largest contributor to overall financial performance.
- Cost and expense categories can be monitored for optimization opportunities.
